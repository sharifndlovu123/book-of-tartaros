The Tartaros Cycle — Sigils & Colours

heraldry of the houses, arms, Synedrion seats, the Keris, and the Roan
A device and a colour palette for every faction in the cycle. Locked colours (the four arms, the Sisters, the Keris) come from the bible's Round Nine; the sigils are proposals for your review, and palettes I invented for entities the bible hadn't coloured carry an proposed tag. Open this file in a browser to see it rendered. Detail lives in ../bible/TARTAROS_CYCLE_BIBLE.md; the cast and family trees are in CHARACTER_MAP.md.
Sigils are line-drawn so they read at any size and can be lifted into standalone SVG files later if you want them as assets. Tell me which devices to keep, swap, or redraw.
