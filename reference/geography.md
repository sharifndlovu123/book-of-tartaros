# The Tartaros Cycle — Geography

*The canonical map of the trilogy: every named world, where it sits, its climate
and weather, what it is for, and who lives there. This file exists because the
worlds were scattered across the bible and two carceral places (Tartaros and the
Harvester) were easy to conflate — so the map is now **set in stone here.** Full
character/world detail still lives in `../bible/TARTAROS_CYCLE_BIBLE.md` (Part I
§8, Part III); this file is the consolidated, authoritative geography.*

**The one thing that caused the confusion — read first:** there are **two**
off-Andra-Prime carceral places, not one. **Tartaros** is the public *prison*
(a cold moon). **The Harvester** is the hidden Cleanse *production facility* (a
hot, two-sun world in its own redacted system). **Book one ends with the five
protagonists held at the Harvester — not at Tartaros.** They are different worlds.

## 2. The four arms are not on one world

The Homonoia's power is **distributed across worlds** — which is *why* it works the
way it does. No single planet holds it; representatives must **travel** to Andra
Prime for the Synedrion, and the Narrine sits at the capital precisely to hold one
narrative across many worlds.

| Arm | Seat / base | Notes |
|-----|-------------|-------|
| **The Vendine** (defense) | **Verdaine** (homeworld) | Hereditary houses; ceremonial aristocracy. The Sisters of the Long Patience are embedded here. |
| **The Asclepi** (medical) | **Elysse** (homeworld) | Also **runs the Harvester** (the hidden facility) — its seat and its secret works are on different worlds. |
| **The Limine** (frontier) | **No single homeworld** — dispersed territories | Operates **Tartaros** and the Cleanse's seven sites; chief named territory is **Vallum** (Raknir Vehl). Power is regional, money mixed. |
| **The Narrine** (communications) | **Andra Prime** (the capital) | Most politically embedded; controls what daily citizens know. Contained the book-one broadcast. |

**Cross-cutting institutions, also on Andra Prime:**
- **The Architect / Homonoia coordination** — Xerxes is politically embedded across
  all four arms; his working seat is the capital. *[Author-decided, pending review:
  the Architect operates from Andra Prime.]*
- **The Synedrion** — meets in **the Tholos**, a circular pre-Homonoia hall on Andra
  Prime (nine chairs; the Keris chair empty). **[CANON]**
- **The Sisters of the Long Patience** — embedded with the Vendine on Verdaine; also
  run **Mother Hessa's clinic on Andra Prime** (lower terraces). **[CANON]**

## 3. The carceral system — set in stone

The piece most prone to being misread. Three distinct places:

### Tartaros — the prison
- **[CANON]** A cold, vacuum-industrial **prison moon orbiting Moloch**. The public
  destination for the convicted: *commit crimes, you are shipped off-world to
  Tartaros.* Run by the **Limine**; **Bastien Orrel is its Warden**.
- **Locations:** Facility Nine (the prison), Wing Seven (the medical wing of the
  abuses), Building Twelve (the children's facility), the underground palace (the
  Asclepi's hidden chamber complex, Elysse-style architecture), the records archive
  (Chayne's duct, where Pira survived).
- **The Undersiders live here** — see below.

### The Undersiders — beneath the prison
- **[Author-decided, pending review — now locked]** The Undersiders are the
  **underclass who live in the under-levels beneath the Tartaros prison complex** —
  a service-and-labor population, the families and forgotten of the carceral world.
  **Sable Reyek's resistance is born here** (her brother Tomas died at Wing Seven).
  Hidden, mutually protective, invisible to Andra Prime's daily citizens — which is
  why a vanished Undersider becomes a *resistance martyr-story*, not a public scandal.

### The Harvester *(public name: the Conservatory)* — the hidden facility
- **[CANON]** The Cleanse's hidden **production facility**: hot, two suns, lush,
  **its own system, redacted from charts**. Run by the **Asclepi**. *Not* Tartaros.
- **Locations:** Pier Three (shuttle dock), the residence wing (**where book one
  ends**), the dining room (the ch10 dinner), Bastien's quarters (the grandfather's
  letter).
- **This is where the five are held at the end of book one** — Sable, Kester, Resh,
  Davour in the residence wing; **Mouse held separately by the Architect's people.**

### Moloch — the anchor
- **[Author-decided, pending review]** A cold gas giant; uninhabitable; Tartaros is
  its prison moon. Defines the remote penal system rather than being settled itself.

### How they connect (and the book-two implication)
- In book one's climax the captured core group is moved **from the Undersider world
  (Tartaros) to the Harvester** (the dinner, Orrel's offer, custody).
- The Harvester ↔ Andra Prime **shuttle route** is established in book one (the ch9
  shuttle left the Harvester's Pier Three; Orrel ordered it shot down "as it reached
  Andra Prime"). **[CANON]**
- **Getting out is interplanetary, not a corridor.** Leaving the Harvester means a
  ship, and ships are controlled by the authorities — the realistic constraint that
  shapes book two's opening (deliberate ship-out vs. earned off-world escape).

## 4. World profiles

### Andra Prime — the home world
- **System/role:** Hegemony capital; the "earth-equivalent" world of daily life.
  Seat of the **Narrine**, the **Synedrion** (the Tholos), and the **Architect**.
- **Climate & weather:** Temperate; genuine seasons; a dense urban microclimate —
  cleaner air and light on the upper terraces, industrial haze and "calm"-numbed
  flatness in the lower ones.
- **Terrain:** A **layered terrace city** — social rank is literally vertical (see §5).
- **Residents:** The daily citizenry ("lowside" in the author's sense — ordinary
  people, *not* the Undersiders); Marella & Cassian & Severine Vren (Narrine);
  Aurelius (Old Houses residence); Lyra Orrel and Lira Vaile (both third terrace);
  Mother Hessa (clinic, lower terraces; from the hollowed middle terraces); **Roan**
  (eastern-quarter syndicate, lower terraces).
- **Key locations:** Cresswell University; the third terrace; the eastern quarter &
  the coffee house; the Tholos; the Information Bureau [TBD]; the Reform Commission [TBD].
- **Story role:** Book two's daily-citizen battlefield — where the Narrine's
  containment lives, where Kester's Roan/Lira thread runs, where the search for Pira
  is staged.

### Tartaros — see §3. *(Prison moon; the Undersiders beneath; Bastien the Warden.)*

### The Harvester — see §3. *(Hidden Cleanse facility; where the five are held.)*

### Verdaine — the Vendine homeworld
- **Climate & weather:** Warm and wet; high humidity, frequent rain, slow rivers that
  flood; long green growing seasons. Lush jungle-and-wetland with great trees.
- **Residents:** The Vendine houses — Orrel, Aemilius, Caradec, and the other senior
  houses; ceremonial aristocracy with spartan internal discipline; the Sisters embedded.
- **Story role:** The defense arm's culture and the duels (the augmented-men scandal
  Hessa has tracked for forty years); ancestral seat of Bastien's house.

### Elysse — the Asclepi homeworld
- **Climate & weather:** Engineered tropical — managed, *too curated to be natural*;
  no real weather, only maintained perfection.
- **Residents:** Asclepi leadership (Aelia Mardin), senior researchers.
- **Story role:** The medical arm's polished face; the architectural source of the
  underground palace glimpsed beneath Tartaros.

### Korya — the Keris homeworld (hidden)
- **Climate & weather:** Cold, *biological* cold. Subarctic tundra above — snow,
  wind, long deep winters; below, geothermally warmed, stable cave-cities.
- **Terrain:** Deceptive — small, primitive-looking mountain settlements above hide
  an industrial, politically coordinated cave-civilization below.
- **Residents:** The Keris bloodline (Imir, Kazimir, Milana, Ivana) and people
  (Nadya's family, ordinary cave-born Keris); **Asham and Pira**.
- **Key locations:** The mountain settlements (what Compact surveys see and leave);
  the cave-cities; Pira's home with Asham [TBD: which cave-city]; the grandfather's
  seat [TBD: which cave-city].
- **Story role:** Thread A's home; the convergence point that closes *The Ward of
  Korya*; the unsurveyed world the protagonists reach without knowing Pira is there.

### Vallum — Raknir Vehl's world *[Author-decided, pending review]*
- **Climate & weather:** Arid and harsh; dust-storm-prone; mineral-rich crust. The
  walls are against the dust as much as against the law.
- **Terrain:** A **walled, rich city-world** with an associated mining **moon**.
- **Residents:** The Vehl family (a large power base); prisoner laborers (cheap, the
  profitable share of the workforce); legitimate miners (fewer); Atticus Vehl's
  contraband sub-syndicate beneath the official one.
- **Story role:** The Limine's most prominent named territory; the hereditary
  **city-key** that *is* the right to govern.

### Sephara — a provincial Hegemony world
- **Climate & weather:** Temperate, unremarkable, provincial.
- **Residents:** Ordinary provincial citizens; **Chayne** kept its municipal
  archives thirty-one years; Tomas Reyek's daughter **Mira**; the guard **Renko's**
  family.
- **Story role:** The plain bureaucratic counterpoint to the arm-seat capitals and
  the hidden Keris world.

### Federation territory
- **[CANON]** A looser alliance that names worlds **plainly** — for founders and
  aspirations, the deliberate opposite of the Hegemony's Latinate-Greek grandeur.
  Hub world **Cairn** (a waymarker of piled stones); members **Halden**, **Auster**.
- **[TBD]** Climates, Adeyemi's homeworld, Yenna's academic contacts, the
  Federation's fuller political shape — to be developed when book two/three requires.

## 5. Andra Prime's terraces — the social ladder

Rank on the capital is **literally vertical**. Three tiers, and they matter for who
notices a disappearance (the realism of book two's opening):

- **Upper terraces / the polished political center** — the institutional-aristocratic
  top: the arms' offices, the Synedrion, the grand residences. Notices through
  *official* channels.
- **Middle terraces** — the respectable middle class. **Lira** (third terrace) and
  **Lyra Orrel** live here; **Hessa** came from a middle district since *hollowed out*
  by the conglomerate. This tier **looks away**; it neither riots nor martyrs.
- **Lower / "bad" terraces** — the underclass of the capital: **Roan's** eastern
  quarter, "calm"-numbed workers, parentless kids, Hessa's clinic. Frontier-terse in
  register; transactional; protective of neighborhood.

**Note on "lowside":** in the author's usage, **lowside = ordinary daily citizens**
(the general surface populace the Narrine manages) — a *different* axis from the
**Undersiders** (the hidden under-prison underclass of Tartaros). Andra Prime's
terraces are the lowside's vertical ladder; the Undersiders are not on it at all.
